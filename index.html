Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this personal Instagram messaging integration ("the App") handles information when it connects an Instagram professional account with a Discord bot.

1. Information the App Accesses

The App may receive information provided through Instagram's APIs, including:

  • Instagram account information such as username and Instagram user ID.
  • Instagram messages sent to the connected Instagram account.
  • Message-related information provided by Instagram through webhooks, such as message IDs and timestamps.

2. How Information Is Used

Information received through Instagram is used to operate the App's messaging features, including:

  • Receiving Instagram direct messages.
  • Forwarding messages to the connected Discord bot.
  • Sending replies from the Discord bot back through Instagram.
  • Applying automated messaging and anti-spam rules configured by the account owner.

The App is a personal project and is not intended to sell, advertise, or otherwise commercially exploit Instagram user information.

3. Sharing of Information

Messages and related information may be transmitted between Instagram and the connected Discord bot in order to provide the messaging bridge.

The App does not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage

The App may temporarily process information received from Instagram while handling messages and webhook events.

Any longer-term storage of messages or other information will only be used where necessary for the App's functionality.

5. Data Deletion

If information received through the App needs to be deleted, a request may be made to the App owner using the contact method listed below.

The App owner may also delete locally stored application data when requested or when it is no longer required for the App's functionality.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the App. Access credentials such as Instagram access tokens and application secrets are kept separate from publicly accessible application code.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's functionality or data handling practices change. The updated version will be published at this URL.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or information handled by the App, you can contact the App owner through the connected Instagram account: @kiz_isonline.

This Privacy Policy applies only to this personal project and its Instagram messaging integration.
